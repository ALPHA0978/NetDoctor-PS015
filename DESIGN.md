# NetDoctor interface

The workbench is for a person trying to isolate a connection problem. It is not a marketing page. Keep the order: choose what to measure, run a check, read the verdict and evidence, then retest or export. Synthetic, measured lab and live results must remain visually and textually distinct.

- Light and dark are paired layouts, not inverted screenshots. Blue is the action color; green, amber and red describe states and always have a text label.
- Use compact headings, equal edges, legible units and restrained surfaces. Avoid decorative data charts.
- Use small press feedback and one 180 ms transition on fresh evidence; reduced motion receives the same result immediately. No scroll choreography, looping display motion or added animation dependency.
- Keep the current report during a run. Show status directly by the action without a fake percentage. Report confirmation before deleting local history.
- Keep controls keyboard reachable, use labelled native form fields, and do not block paste, zoom or native navigation.
- Lab fault-to-control comparison must identify the isolated lab and must not imply household recovery.
